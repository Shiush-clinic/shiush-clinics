Asthma Management at Shiush Clinic

Asthma is a chronic condition where airways become inflamed and narrowed, leading to breathing difficulty, wheezing, and cough.

Common Symptoms

  • Wheezing or whistling sound during breathing
  • Shortness of breath or chest tightness
  • Persistent cough, especially at night or early morning
  • Difficulty performing physical activities

Asthma Triggers to Avoid


Dust

Pollen

Cold Air

Smoke

Exercise

When Should You Visit Us?

  • Using your rescue inhaler more than 2 times a week
  • Disturbed sleep due to coughing or breathlessness
  • Worsening symptoms despite medication
  • Frequent missed school/work due to breathing trouble
