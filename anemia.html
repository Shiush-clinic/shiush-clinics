Anemia in Women – Awareness & Care

Anemia is a condition where the body doesn't have enough healthy red blood cells to carry oxygen to tissues. It's especially common in women due to menstruation, pregnancy, and nutritional deficiencies.

Causes in Women

  • Iron deficiency (most common)
  • Vitamin B12 or Folate deficiency
  • Heavy menstrual bleeding
  • Pregnancy-related increased demand
  • Postpartum blood loss

Common Symptoms

  • Pale skin, tiredness, and weakness
  • Shortness of breath and dizziness
  • Cold hands and feet
  • Craving ice, clay, or dirt (Pica)
  • Hair loss or brittle nails

Management at SHIUSH Clinics

  • Hemoglobin & Iron Studies (CBC, Ferritin)
  • IV or oral iron therapy
  • Folic acid and B12 supplementation
  • Diet counseling – iron-rich foods (leafy greens, jaggery, dates)
  • Pregnancy anemia protocol (as per WHO guidelines)
