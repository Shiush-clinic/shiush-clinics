Devi

Devi, a 45-year-old woman, visited SHIUSH Clinics with pain, swelling, and discoloration in her finger due to a rat bite. Over a few days, the wound developed into a subungual abscess. With timely diagnosis and proper treatment, she recovered completely, regaining full use of her finger and expressing heartfelt gratitude.
