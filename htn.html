Hypertension (HTN) Management at Shiush Clinic

High Blood Pressure (Hypertension) is a silent killer. It often has no symptoms, yet it quietly damages the heart, brain, and kidneys over time.

Common Symptoms

  • Headache, dizziness
  • Blurred vision
  • Fatigue or confusion
  • Chest pain or shortness of breath

Our Services for HTN

  • Daily Blood Pressure Monitoring
  • ECG and basic cardiac evaluation
  • Lifestyle & Diet Counseling
  • Medication Adjustment & Adherence Support
  • Renal, lipid & sugar checkups

Service Availability

💊 Regular BP Checkup
📈 Diet & Lifestyle Plans
🩺 Medication Review
🧪 Lab Monitoring

When to Consult

  • BP consistently over 140/90 mmHg
  • Family history of HTN
  • Associated diabetes or kidney issues
  • Stress, fatigue, or vision problems
