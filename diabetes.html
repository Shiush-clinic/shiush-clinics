Diabetes Management at Shiush Clinic

Diabetes is a long-term condition that affects how your body turns food into energy. It requires continuous care and healthy living.

Common Symptoms

  • Excessive thirst and frequent urination
  • Unexplained weight loss
  • Fatigue, blurred vision, slow-healing wounds
  • Tingling or numbness in hands/feet

Diabetes Services We Offer

  • Blood Sugar Testing (FBS, PP, HbA1c)
  • Diet & Physical Activity Planning
  • Medication & Insulin Initiation Guidance
  • Diabetic Foot & Eye Screening
  • BP, Lipid, Renal Monitoring
  • Education on Hypoglycemia & Complication Prevention

When to See a Doctor

  • Family history of diabetes with weight gain
  • High fasting sugar or HbA1c > 6.5%
  • Symptoms of tiredness or vision changes
  • Foot problems or repeated infections
