Tuberculosis (TB) Management at SHIUSH Clinics

Tuberculosis (TB) is a serious but treatable disease caused by Mycobacterium tuberculosis. Early diagnosis and consistent treatment are crucial to recovery.

Common Symptoms

  • Persistent cough lasting more than 2 weeks
  • Blood in sputum (hemoptysis)
  • Weight loss and fatigue
  • Night sweats and fever
  • Chest pain or breathlessness

Diagnosis Methods

  • Sputum AFB Microscopy
  • CBNAAT (GeneXpert)
  • Chest X-Ray
  • Mantoux Test

DOTS Program & Treatment

SHIUSH Clinics follows the National Tuberculosis Elimination Program (NTEP) and offers:

  • Free anti-TB medication under DOTS (Directly Observed Treatment Short-course)
  • Regular follow-ups and sputum checks
  • Counseling to ensure adherence and reduce side effects

Important Advice

  • Always complete the full course (6 months or longer)
  • Maintain cough hygiene – use a mask and dispose of tissues safely
  • Eat high-protein, calorie-rich food
  • Separate utensils and bedding if active TB is diagnosed
