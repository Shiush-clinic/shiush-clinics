Mental Health Matters at Shiush Clinic

Mental health includes emotional, psychological, and social well-being. It affects how we think, feel, and act, and it is just as important as physical health.

Common Mental Health Issues We Address

  • Anxiety Disorders
  • Depression & Mood Swings
  • Insomnia & Sleep Disorders
  • Postpartum Depression
  • Stress & Burnout
  • Teenage Emotional Issues

Services We Provide

  • Confidential Counselling Sessions
  • Stress & Anger Management Techniques
  • Behavioral Therapy for Children/Teens
  • Medication & Follow-up (if required)
  • Referral to Psychiatrists when needed

When to Seek Help

  • Constant sadness or irritability
  • Fear, worry, or panic attacks
  • Changes in eating or sleeping habits
  • Withdrawal from social interaction
  • Thoughts of self-harm or hopelessness
