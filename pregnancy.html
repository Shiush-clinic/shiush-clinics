Pregnancy Care at SHIUSH Clinic

Our clinic offers comprehensive Antenatal Care (ANC) ensuring the well-being of both mother and baby. Early and regular visits help detect and prevent complications.

First Visit Includes

  • Complete history taking
  • General physical & systemic examination
  • Baseline investigations (Hb, Blood Group, HIV, HBsAg, VDRL, Urine R/M)
  • Early obstetric ultrasound
  • Iron, calcium and folic acid supplements initiation

Subsequent ANC Visits

  • Weight, BP, fetal growth monitoring
  • Second trimester ultrasound (Anomaly Scan)
  • Tetanus toxoid immunizations
  • Diabetes screening (GTT), Hemoglobin monitoring
  • Counseling on danger signs, diet & hygiene

Important Tips for Pregnant Women

  • Eat a balanced, iron-rich diet
  • Stay hydrated and well-rested
  • Avoid self-medication
  • Report any bleeding, swelling, or decreased fetal movement immediately
