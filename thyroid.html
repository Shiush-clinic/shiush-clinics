Thyroid Health at Shiush Clinic

Thyroid Disorders such as Hypothyroidism and Hyperthyroidism can lead to weight issues, mood swings, fatigue, and more. Early diagnosis and proper treatment are key.

Common Symptoms

  • Weight gain or loss
  • Fatigue or nervousness
  • Hair fall, dry skin
  • Menstrual irregularities
  • Constipation or diarrhea
