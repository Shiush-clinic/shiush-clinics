Meet the Founder

Dr. Nitish Shukla, MBBS

Dr. Nitish Shukla, MBBS, is the founder and driving force behind SHIUSH Clinics. With a vision to make quality primary healthcare accessible, affordable, and compassionate, he established the clinic to serve people with dedication and sincerity. His mission goes beyond just treating illness — he believes in patient education, preventive care, and building lasting trust with every individual who walks in.

He believes in building strong doctor-patient relationships based on trust, empathy, and education. Whether you're visiting for a regular checkup, chronic disease management, or general health advice, Dr. Shukla ensures that you are heard, understood, and treated with the highest standards of medical ethics and care.
